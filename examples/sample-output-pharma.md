# Sample Output: Pharma Commercial Positioning

An abbreviated example of a pharma run. The product, data and competitors are fictional. Real output uses supplied or cited evidence only and marks missing figures with `[DATA NEEDED]`.

---

## Research Context

| Field | Detail |
| ----- | ------ |
| **Product** | Brand X. Once-monthly subcutaneous injection for a chronic inflammatory condition. |
| **Indication** | Moderate to severe adults after inadequate response to first-line oral therapy |
| **Stage** | Launch year |
| **Evidence** | Two placebo-controlled phase 3 trials. No head-to-head data. |
| **Competitors** | Two established biologics dosed every two weeks, plus a pipeline oral agent |
| **Priority customers** | Specialists, commercial payers, specialty pharmacy |

---

## Module P1: Differentiator to Claim Map (excerpt)

| Differentiator | Draft Claim | Claim Type | Risk Flag |
| -------------- | ----------- | ---------- | --------- |
| Monthly dosing | "One injection a month" | On-label promotional | None if consistent with label dosing |
| Efficacy | "Superior to Competitor A" | Not usable | Comparative. Needs head-to-head data. Cross-trial only. |
| Efficacy | "[DATA NEEDED: primary endpoint result] versus placebo" | On-label promotional | Requires fair balance |

## Module P2: Stakeholder Map (excerpt)

| Stakeholder | What They Need to See | Main Barrier |
| ----------- | --------------------- | ------------ |
| Specialists | Efficacy in patients who failed oral therapy | Comfort with established biologics |
| Payers | Budget impact and fit within step therapy | Two existing biologics already on formulary |
| Patients | Fewer injections, support getting started | Cost and prior authorization delays |

## Module P4: Positioning Statement (excerpt)

For specialists treating adults with moderate to severe disease after oral therapy, Brand X is the once-monthly biologic that reduces treatment burden while delivering [DATA NEEDED: primary endpoint benefit] because of [mechanism of action, as stated in label].

*Draft for internal strategy. All claims require medical, legal and regulatory review before use.*
